SandStat — beach volleyball score keeper & stats

Keep score and stats for 2-player beach volleyball from the sideline — one tap per rally.

Download on the App Store Get it on Google Play

iPhone · iPad · Android

  • Score every rally with one tap and tag the player and the skill — kills, shots, aces, blocks, digs, errors.
  • Built for the beach: two players a side, court-switch reminders, and hard-driven vs. shot stats.
  • Player stats for each match and across matches, plus a PDF match report to share.
  • Live score: turn on broadcast and share the code — family and teammates can follow from anywhere.
  • Works offline, no account needed. Available in 11 languages.

Some features require SandStat Pro, a one-time in-app purchase.
